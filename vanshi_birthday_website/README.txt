VANSHI BIRTHDAY WEBSITE
========================

Files:
- index.html        -> open this in a browser
- assets/            -> the 4 photos + birthday video

How to use:
1. Keep the folder structure exactly as it is.
2. Open index.html in Chrome/Safari/Edge.
3. For phone use, transfer the whole folder/zip and open index.html.
4. The site works offline because all photos and the video are included locally.

To publish it online later, upload the whole folder to a static website host.
